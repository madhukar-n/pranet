I completed my BTech in Computer Science from Jamia Hamdard, New Delhi, where I developed an interest in security through my project on an Artificial Immune System-based Intrusion Detection System.

Currently, my research focuses on SDN security, particularly detecting topology poisoning attacks in which malicious applications fabricate non-existent network links.

Outside academics, I actively participate in sports and have won a silver medal in powerlifting and a bronze medal in cricket at IIT Hyderabad’s inter-department tournaments.
